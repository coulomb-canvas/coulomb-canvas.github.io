### Asteroids

Asteroids are mostly in the asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. It's quite possible that this asteroid belt is actually a failed planet, where a bunch of rocks were about to get together to form a planet (more on that next time) but instead got obliterated by Jupiter's shear aura.

They are less dense than rocks, meaning they either are made of ice or have high porosity (i.e. they are not so much single objects, but rather rubble piles which have kinda been smooshed together).

### Meteorites

*Chondrites* are fragments of *c-type asteroids*, which contain spherical chondrules of primordial material from the formation of the solar system. They are deficient in volatiles (similar composition to bulk earth)

*Achondrites* are from *s-type asteroids* and don't have condrules, since they likely melted. They are rocky and iron deficient. Mantle of a differentiated and disrupted object.

These last ones don't have a particularly interesting name, they are just called *iron meteorites*, and they come from M-type asteroids, the core of a differentiated and disrupted object.

### Comets

Small icy bodies from Oort clouds which are occationally thrown into the solar system on highly elliptical orbits. Distinct ions (ionised gas carried by solar wind) and dust tail (Keplarian orbits and radiation pressure). They have very low densities, meaning they are very porous, like powder snow with dark surfaces of dust and carbon compounds ("dirty snowballs")
