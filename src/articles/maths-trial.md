I believe in God, there was some one guy who said "Mathematics is the language with which God created the universe". That makes this section seem way more philosophical than it probably is. Anyway, you get to learn the language of the universe. By the time you're done, you will be able to order a coffee and ask for directions in God's language.

People often say, if you want to study maths at university, don't study maths at university. I don't know if you've seen the stuff they do in maths, but it's certainly strange. Things like analysis, or proving that 0 is not 1, or having to verify every individual step of a calculation is valid.
